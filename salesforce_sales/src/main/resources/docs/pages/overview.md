# Overview

* The Krista Salesforce Sales Module Extension connects to the Salesforce system and its Sales module and performs various operations. The Salesforce Krista extension supports business operations and cloud solution for Sales activities.

#### Prerequisite
* To connect to the Salesforce Sales system, you must have a Salesforce Developer account.

#### Note
* The Salesforce Krista extension supports entities such as Opportunity, Asset, Lead, Account, Event, User, Pricebook Entry, Quote, and Sales Pipeline Summary.
